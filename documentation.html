KNOW THE DIFFERENCE

What each property record is used for

A Khata is used for municipal property records. An Encumbrance Certificate lists registered transactions for a chosen period. Registration and utility-account changes serve different purposes.

No single record by itself establishes clear legal title. A qualified advocate should carry out title verification when required.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Is the generated checklist a final government requirement?

    No. The list is a preliminary guide based on the service and situation you choose. The competent authority can require additional papers or a different procedure.

    Can TrustDocs guarantee a Khata or registration approval?

    No. The relevant government office determines requirements, processing times, applicable fees and approval. TrustDocs can help prepare and coordinate the application.

    Can an Encumbrance Certificate replace a legal title search?

    No. An EC reflects registered transactions for the period requested. A full title review requires an independent qualified advocate.
