APPLICATION DOCUMENTS

Documents lenders may ask for

Requirements vary by bank, income type, borrower profile and property.

Identity and income

PAN, address proof, bank statements, salary slips, Form 16 or ITRs, depending on employment type.

Property information

Sale agreement or allotment letter, ownership records, approvals and project details, where required.

Loan sanction terms

Confirm the sanctioned amount, rate, fees, valuation conditions and disbursal schedule before acceptance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the EMI calculator a loan quote?

No. It is an illustrative calculation based on the amount, annual rate and tenure selected. Actual lender offers depend on eligibility, charges, rate changes and sanction terms.

Does a bank listed here have a partnership with TrustDocs?

Not necessarily. Listed institutions are examples of Indian home loan providers. Inclusion alone is not evidence of an authorised partnership or guaranteed loan approval.

What should I compare besides the interest rate?

Compare processing charges, fixed or floating rate terms, loan tenure, prepayment conditions, insurance, disbursal conditions and the total repayment amount.
