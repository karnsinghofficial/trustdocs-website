Frequently Asked Questions

Does TrustDocs help with buying as well as investing in a home?

Yes. The Real Estate Advisory service focuses on residential home buying and property investment in Bengaluru. It includes property comparison, project information and site-visit coordination.

Can TrustDocs approve a home loan?

No. A bank or housing finance company decides eligibility, sanction, interest rates and disbursal. TrustDocs can help you understand the application requirements and compare important terms.

Is an EC or Khata enough to establish property title?

No. These records serve different purposes. Ask an independent qualified advocate to conduct legal title verification for the property you are considering.

Does the website store my enquiry?

The form prepares a message for you to review in WhatsApp. This version does not submit form data to a TrustDocs web database. If you send the message, WhatsApp and TrustDocs receive the information you choose to share.
