Privacy and enquiries

What happens when you use the enquiry form or the planning tools on this website.

On this pageInformation you choose to shareWhatsApp handoffPlanning toolsExternal servicesContact

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, telephone number, service requirement, property area and optional details. You can review the message before sending it. Please do not include passwords, ID document numbers or sensitive financial records in a general enquiry.

WhatsApp handoff

The website prepares a WhatsApp message on your device. This version does not send the enquiry to a TrustDocs website database. If you choose to send the message in WhatsApp, the message and any information you provide are received through WhatsApp and can be seen by TrustDocs. WhatsApp's terms and privacy practices apply.

Property finder and documentation assistant

The property finder prepares a summary of your choices. The documentation assistant creates a preliminary checklist based on the service and information you select. Both tools run in your browser. No personal document upload is requested.

External services

Telephone and email links use your device's respective apps. The website may load fonts or media from a third-party provider if those are configured when published. Any future analytics, CRM or hosted form service should be covered by an updated privacy notice before activation.

Contact TrustDocs

For enquiries about information shared with TrustDocs, email contact@trustdocs.in or call +91 9180249063 or +91 8271259777.

If you have questions about your enquiry or the information you shared, contact TrustDocs using the details above.
