BEFORE YOU COMMIT

What to review before booking

A project brochure is only one part of the decision. These are useful areas to examine before paying a booking amount.

Project and unit

Location, site access, floor plan, construction or possession stage and the specific unit offered.

Purchase cost

Base price, taxes, registration expenses, maintenance charges and the payment schedule.

Property records

Title documents, Khata, EC, sanctioned plans and applicable RERA details. Consult an independent advocate for legal verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit a requirement without choosing a project?

Yes. Share your preferred area, approximate budget, configuration and timeline using the property finder. It prepares a brief for discussion without showing unverified listings.

Are property returns or future appreciation guaranteed?

No. Residential property investments carry market, construction, liquidity and other risks. Review the total purchase cost, local conditions and your holding period before deciding.

Does TrustDocs verify legal ownership or title?

TrustDocs assists with applicable record requests. Independent title verification should be undertaken by a qualified property advocate.
